Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and a password. When you use Spendly, we also store the expenses you enter, including the amount, category, date and description. We do not ask for bank account or card details, and you should avoid entering them in expense descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, to store your expenses, and to show you summaries and category breakdowns. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database. Passwords are stored in hashed form and never in plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may send a request to Google when you visit the site. We do not share your account or expense data with any other third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please get in touch with the Spendly team at the contact address provided on the website.
